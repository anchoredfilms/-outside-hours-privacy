# Privacy Policy — Outside Hours

Last updated: 09/11/2026

Outside Hours is designed with your privacy as a core value.

## Data we collect
**None.** Outside Hours does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no network communication.

## Data stored on your device
All information you enter — member names, goals, logged sessions, streaks, and settings — is stored **locally on your device only** using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or using the in-app "Reset all data" option permanently deletes this data.

## Photo library access
The app requests permission to save your progress card image to your device's Photos library only when you tap "Save image" or "Share." The image is created on your device and saved locally. We do not upload, transmit, or view your photos.

## Children's privacy
Outside Hours is safe for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Third-party services
None.
